QoL Toolkit 0.1.6 for WoW Forever 1.60.1

Added

- Class Portraits. Use full class-themed player and target frames for all nine classes. Full frames are enabled by default and can be switched back to portrait ornaments for each frame. Paladin frames include class-specific resource colors and healing indicators.
- Bags. Optionally sell poor-quality items and repair your own gear when visiting a merchant. A coin marks items eligible for automatic sale.
- Effect Reminders. Set up class-specific reminders that warn when none of the effects in a selected family is active.

Improved

- Flight Times. Customize the timer ornaments and faction emblems. The settings preview is larger, and numeric fields support precise adjustments.
- Flight Times. Smooth the in-flight progress bar and show a single labeled travel time for known routes on the flight map.
- Bags and settings. Open backpack options using the Blizzard-style gear button and resize the settings window with the native resize grip.

Fixed

- Flight Times. Reuse recorded durations after a client update and keep the preview stable while editing sliders or entering values.
