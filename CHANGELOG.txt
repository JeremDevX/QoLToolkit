QoL Toolkit 0.1.3 for WoW Forever 1.60.1

Added

- Standalone settings window. Open every module from the minimap icon or /qt, with grouped tabs and subtabs, a draggable title, resizing, and Escape to close.
- Flight Times. Measure complete taxi routes, show personal durations and available community estimates, and export measurements without character names.
- Target Portrait. Show a circular class medallion with a class-colored border beside the native target portrait.
- Session Journal. Save up to 100 recent sessions per character. Review all-time, 7-day, and 30-day totals, plot XP and net gold over a session, and compare hourly rates across sessions.
- Settings search. Find controls from the dashboard and jump to the matching setting, including controls inside collapsed sections.
- Talent Planner. Duplicate builds and share them through a copy-and-paste import and export format. Imports validate the full plan before saving a new build.
- Bags. Customize colors for each rarity, quest items, and crafting reagents. Enable each category separately and match item glows to its color.

Improved

- Dashboard. Find tools under Character, Interface, Customization, and Tools. Tooltips and distance share a settings page. Journal tracking and reminder selection each have their own section separate from display options.
- Spell Rank Guard. Refresh warnings when you learn a spell or open a trainer, with a 30-second background scan.
- Restored the QoL Toolkit minimap artwork.

Fixed

- Flight Times. Discard interrupted routes even when early landing is requested before the first periodic taxi-state check.
- Settings search. Include Flight Times and its recording and export controls.
- Target Distance. Keep range estimates when a direct measurement is unavailable and use consistent estimates when the target is also under the cursor.
- Session Journal. Restore search results for all journal stats and display options.
- Advanced Settings. Keep minimum and maximum nameplate scale and opacity consistent when resetting settings.
- Diagnostics. Include the Blizzard API name in captures when an addon action is blocked or forbidden and the client supplies the name.
